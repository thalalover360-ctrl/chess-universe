1. Stalemate (Draw Rules)

Chess me stalemate match ko Draw kar deta hai. Yeh 4 scenarios me hota hai:

  • No Legal Move: King par check nhi hai, par player ke paas koi legal chal bachi hi nahi.
  • Insufficient Material: K vs K, K+B vs K, ya K+N vs K (mate karna namumkin).
  • Threefold Repetition: Same board position 3 baar repeat ho jaye.
  • 50-Move Rule: Lagataar 50 chal tak na koi pawn chala na koi piece capture hua.
👑

2. Pawn Promotion

Jab tera Pawn board ke aakhri square (White ke liye 8th rank, Black ke liye 1st rank) par pahuchta hai, toh wo turant promote hota hai:

Queen (♛)
Rook (♜)
Bishop (♝)
Knight (♞)
⚔️

3. En Passant (Special Capture)

Agar opponent ka Pawn apni shuruati jagah se 2 steps chal kar tere Pawn ke barabar me aata hai, toh tu agle hi move me use diagonally capture kar sakta hai jaise usne sirf 1 step chala ho. Yeh sirf turant agle turn par valid hota hai!
